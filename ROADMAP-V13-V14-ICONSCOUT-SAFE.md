# ROYAL DESIGN — V13–V14 (IconScout-safe workflow)

Date: 2026-11-08
Repository: `Ramistar900/royal-design`
Target branch: `main`

## Purpose

Use IconScout only as a visual discovery and inspiration reference. ROYAL DESIGN's V13 and V14 deliverables must be independently authored ROYAL assets, not copied or repackaged IconScout downloads.

Official policy references (re-check the exact asset license at acquisition time):
- https://iconscout.com/licenses
- https://help.iconscout.com/iconscout-digital-physical-license-policy
- https://iconscout.io/legal/license-and-usage-agreement

## V13 — Original 2D icon system

- Scope: original 2D vector icons designed by/for ROYAL DESIGN.
- Styles: Flat and Glass as distinct visual systems; color and gold variants only where they improve meaning and consistency.
- Deliverables: editable original SVG source, optimized PNG exports when needed, Android VectorDrawable exports when validated, catalog metadata and preview sheets.
- IconScout may be used to study broad visual trends and identify functional concepts only. Do not trace, copy, recolor, lightly modify, or reproduce a specific IconScout asset or distinctive composition.
- Do not download or commit IconScout source files into the reusable library.

## V14 — Original 3D icon system

- Scope: independently authored 3D icons and renders.
- Styles: 3D Flat and 3D Glass; controlled lighting, consistent camera, transparent background where practical, and color/gold variants only when useful.
- Deliverables: original editable project/source where available, transparent high-resolution renders, catalog metadata and previews.
- IconScout may be used for broad inspiration only. Do not copy or derive a near-identical model, render, material setup, or distinctive composition from a specific marketplace asset.
- Do not download or commit IconScout source files into the reusable library.

## Motion policy

Motion remains in the already planned independent V12 track. V14 does not absorb or replace V12. Any future animated original ROYAL icons must have a static fallback and reduced-motion behavior.

## Legal and provenance gates

For every candidate:
1. Record whether it is an original ROYAL creation or a third-party reference.
2. Never place third-party reference files in the distributable icon library.
3. If a third-party asset is ever proposed for direct inclusion in a finished application, verify the exact asset license, account/license entitlement, permitted project scope, redistribution/storage restrictions, attribution, and end-product distribution model first.
4. Do not assume a generic free download, daily freebie, or paid subscription grants rights to redistribute source files or build a competing icon library.
5. Exclude logos, trademarks, and branded marks unless the rights holder explicitly authorizes the intended use.
6. Preserve the original V2–V12 assets; no replacement or deletion without inventory, dependency checks, archive, and user approval.

## Quality gates

- Distinctive original design; recognizable at small sizes.
- Consistent geometry, stroke, perspective, lighting and spacing within each family.
- Check light/dark backgrounds, transparency, clipping and export quality.
- Validate SVG/XML and generated platform resources.
- Add only after preview and automated checks pass.
- Never mark a batch imported or complete until actual files are committed and verified.
